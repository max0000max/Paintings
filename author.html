BIOGRAPHY

Franz Wertvollen grew up between Kazakhstan and Paris and studied in France, earning a degree in Human Sciences, Art and Culture, specializing in Information and Communication.

He founded his own acting school in 2011 and published his first book in 2013. Since then, he has written more than fifteen historical novels. Today, Wertvollen lives and works in Los Angeles.
