Franz wertvollen

— Writer, philosopher
and visual artist

Art should show you how splendid life can truly be.

It ought to take you, drag you out of the slimy mire of your skull,
and unfold your soul so you can see: ’My God! I have so much
room to grow. Now I see what heights are out there for me. I
never imagined one could feel so free!’

Yet art makes demands. You need to engage your heart”.
